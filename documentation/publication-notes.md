## German legal notice

Checked against the primary legal text on 19 September 2026. [Section 5 DDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html) sets information duties for businesslike digital services normally offered for remuneration. When it applies, the required information includes the provider's name, establishment address and a means of direct electronic contact including email.

Whether this specific job-search portfolio falls within that provision is not established by the repository. A lack of advertising or payment does not, by itself, settle the question. The dashboard's author credit and GitHub link are not a claim of compliance with an Impressum obligation. Other applicable information duties should also be checked before a compliance conclusion is made.

No home address, placeholder address or inferred personal contact information has been published. If a legal review establishes that an Impressum is required, the owner must supply and approve the details to publish before one is added.
